# Content-rights reporting and review

Yomirra is an independent multi-source reader. This page provides a project-maintainer intake channel for concerns about manga/manhwa/manhua content, translations, artwork, logos, or linked third-party sources.

## Submit a report

Open a GitHub issue using the [Content Rights report template](https://github.com/rzqllh/Yomirra/issues/new?template=content-rights.yml). This is a **public** issue: do not include private addresses, identity documents, credentials, private correspondence, or copies of full copyrighted chapters.

Include, where available:
- The exact Yomirra URL and the external source URL involved;
- The title/content you believe is affected;
- The nature of your rights or authorization to report;
- The requested action (for example: review, disable an adapter entry, correct attribution or remove a link);
- Non-sensitive evidence sufficient for an initial assessment.

If confidential evidence is necessary, state that it is available privately **without posting it in a public issue**. Maintainers should arrange an appropriate private contact channel before requesting that evidence.

## Maintainer review workflow

1. Acknowledge the report and identify the relevant URL, provider, feature and affected material.
2. Review the actual route and source handling, distinguishing Yomirra-hosted files, proxied/cache content, third-party links, and user-imported files.
3. Preserve only necessary non-sensitive incident evidence and assess an interim protective measure where warranted; do not publish potentially sensitive evidence.
4. Escalate unresolved rights and legal questions to an authorized maintainer and qualified counsel. Evaluate remediation under applicable law and platform terms.
5. Record the decision and remediation status in the issue where it is safe and appropriate to do so. Protect reporters' and users' personal information.

This workflow does not promise a statutory deadline, validate ownership automatically, or assert compliance with any jurisdiction's copyright notice procedure. The project owner must designate an accountable reviewer and approve any final legal policy or takedown procedure. **Reporting availability does not itself constitute legal clearance.**
