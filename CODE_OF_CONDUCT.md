# Code of Conduct

Yomirra welcomes contributors who communicate respectfully and work in good faith.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Discuss code and behavior rather than attacking people.
- Assume mistakes before malice.
- Accept technical disagreement without harassment.
- Respect privacy, source terms, and intellectual-property boundaries.
- Give credit for ideas and contributions.
- Help keep issues and pull requests focused.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sexualized or degrading comments directed at contributors.
- Deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Repeatedly ignoring maintainer boundaries or project scope.
- Submitting stolen code, credentials, or copyrighted content without permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer. Do not post sensitive reports in public issues.

This policy applies in repository discussions, reviews, project chat, and other spaces where someone is representing Yomirra.
